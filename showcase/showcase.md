# Fathom

Fathom is a deep-sea maze chase where your only map is the light you carry. Every corridor begins hidden, and each dive asks you to find a route by moving carefully and listening for what moves beyond your sight.

Grazing plankton brightens your glow and widens the view. A sonar pulse travels through open corridors, sketching the maze and briefly marking hunters, while an ink cloud can break the line of sight of the light-seeking predators.

Three creatures hunt in different ways. The Lanternjaw follows your light beneath an amber disguise, the eyeless Gloamfin listens for movement and answers with its own pings, and the Flarefish disappears between sudden blooms that reveal a wide slice of trench.

Clear every mote to descend into a deeper maze. Each life is a chance to read the dark a little better, and every bright glimmer could be a harmless drifter—or a hunter coming closer.
